Grid/84 Background documents

The working papers

Grid/84 is a playback engine for the history and doctrine of strategic nuclear weapons, and it publishes what it stands on. These are the research dossiers behind the models: the sources read, the figures found, the disputes left open, and the numbers in wide circulation that do not survive checking.

Last revised 10 September 2026 Return to the atlas

Why these exist

A model of a nuclear exchange is a chain of numbers, and a chain is worth the weakest link in it. Every figure the engine uses carries a source and an evidence tier, but a tier in a tooltip cannot show how the figure was arrived at, which papers disagree about it, or what had to be assumed when nothing was published at all. These documents do that work at length.

They follow one convention throughout: a quoted figure is read from the source, not from a summary of it; a composite is labelled as a composite; and where a widely repeated number cannot be traced to a paper, that is stated rather than quietly dropped.
